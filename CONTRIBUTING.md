# Contributing

CA-owned material is available under [MIT](LICENSE-MIT) **OR**
[Apache-2.0](LICENSE-APACHE), at the recipient's option. See
[Licensing](docs/LICENSING.md) for scope, existing MIT permissions, and the
recorded provenance limits.

By intentionally submitting an original contribution for inclusion, you offer
it under **both** licenses, so recipients may choose either. You retain your
copyright; this is not a copyright assignment. Submit only material you have
authority to license, including any necessary employer or other rights-holder
permission. Do not promise rights or patent grants on another author's behalf.

Identify third-party material before inclusion. Provide its upstream URL,
version or commit, source paths, original license and copyright notices, and
any changes you made. Preserve its terms and applicable NOTICE text. Clearly
identify material that cannot be offered under both project licenses so its
compatibility and scope can be reviewed. Existing MIT-only permissions are
preserved; importing that material does not create an Apache patent grant from
its original author. Do not copy implementation source from the excluded
oracle/reference archives.

Keep changes focused and describe what changed and how it was verified.
Follow [AGENTS](AGENTS.md), [Development](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md), and
[Testing](TESTING.md) for repository conventions and applicable checks.
When dependencies or distributed assets change, update
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), the
[dependency inventory](licenses/dependency-inventory.json), and generated
release notices using the
[notice generator](tools/generate_dependency_notices.py) as applicable.
